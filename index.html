The Collection

Eight marks. One frontier.

Each TrailMark is cast to represent a single landmark on the route — not a stage number, but a place worth stopping for. Collect them in order and the route draws itself.

Display

Made to be finished, and hung.

Complete the set and TrailMarks becomes a single piece — eight marks mounted together, the route running unbroken from Bowness-on-Solway to Segedunum.

Each frame is numbered and dated to your own walk, not a factory run.

The Process

Walk. Collect. Display.

Three stages, in order — because that's the order the wall puts you in.

I

Walk

Follow the Hadrian's Wall Path at your own pace — a weekend section or the full eighty miles, coast to coast.

II

Collect

Scan the QR code at each landmark to unlock and receive your TrailMark, cast in oxidised bronze.

III

Display

Mount your set as it grows, or frame the finished eight — a record of a route you actually walked.

Digital Journey

The wall remembers what the metal can't.

Every TrailMark carries a QR code linking to that section's digital journal — upload photos, log the day's weather, read what other walkers left behind at the same milestone.

  • Live progress across all eight sections
  • Photo uploads tied to the exact landmark
  • A community gallery for each stretch of wall
  • A digital passport stamped as you go
